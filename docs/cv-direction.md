# CV direction contract

Mode: Read. Approved precise extension of an established visual world. No
concept tournament or raster comp: the responsive HTML is the review mockup.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One complete professional record, readable at two depths: recent
achievements first, complete earlier roles and leadership below. Group Deloitte
promotions while preserving every title and date. The content revision retains
every substantive sourced achievement bullet, including earlier work.

**OWN-WORLD:** Existing white canvas on zinc-50, zinc-800 headings, zinc-600 body
text, restrained teal links, sans-serif type, and familiar small avatar/pill
navigation. Respect incumbent dark-theme conventions. No new brand system.

**STORY:** A visitor understands Stephen's current customer-deployment work,
follows his progression through Agentnoon and Deloitte, then finds earlier
experience, leadership, education, skills, languages, and recognition. They can
download the supplied redacted résumé or visit Projects/Contact.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Existing site header with CV active; Experience heading;
short professional introduction; résumé action; dated opening Dayforce entry.
Section wayfinding provides a route through the long document. Mobile reflows
dates above entries and retains the full role inventory.

**FORM:** User-approved chronology and grouped promotions, inherited site shell.
Concept-seed is intentionally inapplicable to this approved narrow structure.

**FINISH:** Finished HTML, verified desktop/mobile captures, working controls
and downloads, one mechanical detector pass and independent finish review.
Document the inherited identity and review evidence without changing DESIGN.md
or creating a new brand system.
